How it works

  • Letters: hold a sign still for 1.5 seconds to add it. The space and del signs, or the buttons above, finish a word or remove a letter.
  • Words: hold a sign for half a second. It knows
  • Tutor: copy the outline on the camera. You get a green light when your sign matches.
